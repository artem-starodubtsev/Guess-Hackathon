How the search works

One 144D descriptor, three independently compared blocks. Pattern combines three fabric regions through a learned DINO projection. Shape uses a metadata-trained projection of the CNN embedding; metadata is not used to rerank results. Palette uses color transport in Lab; pattern and shape use cosine distance. Fixed distance scales make the weights comparable.

Photo views with the same style and color are grouped. The best-matching view supplies the product score and thumbnail; different colors remain separate. The final distance is their weighted average. Lower is closer. Sliders express relative emphasis, not a minimum similarity requirement. Ties within quantized 0.000001 distance buckets use a stable item order.
